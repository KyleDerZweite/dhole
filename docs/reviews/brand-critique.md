# Brand shortlist critique

Reviewed on 2026-09-05 against the [name research](brand-names.md),
[animal study](brand-animals.md), and [comparison board](brand-board.html).
This is a critique of the researched shortlist. Dhole remains the product name.

Rookport is the strongest wordmark to test. It is compact, easy to say, and
pairs well with a rook profile. "Port" gives the name some connection meaning,
but the words alone still do not explain agent coordination or provider
administration. Keep the plain product description beside it:

> Manage AI provider access and coordinate your agents.

The main objection is substantial. [Rook](https://rook.io/) is an established
Kubernetes storage project in the same broad infrastructure audience. The
complete Rookport name helps distinguish it, but does not erase that
association. Avoid shortening it to Rook or using chess and storage imagery.
The preliminary GitHub/npm checks are not trademark or domain clearance.

Packrail better suggests agents working together and keeps the dhole animal
without its pronunciation problem. It also sounds like logistics, package
handling, or a scheduler. Those are design judgments to test with people who
have not seen the dashboard. There is a language-specific reservation too.
[Duden's entry for "Pack"](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Pack_Poebel_Gesindel)
labels the German noun "salopp abwertend" and defines it as a disparaging term
for a group of people. That does not establish how German speakers will read
the English compound, but it is a real reason to test it before selecting it.
The Duden entry was retrieved directly for this review.

Otterport is approachable and offers a clear rounded mascot. It ranks a distant
third because [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) already operates in AI and
[OtterLink](https://github.com/lihy11/OtterLink) overlaps directly with remote
coding-agent access. A friendly animal does not compensate for that proximity.

My recommendation is to compare Rookport and Packrail at the same size in the
actual sidebar and sign-in page. Ask whether a new reader can say the name,
remember it, and describe the product after seeing the one-sentence description.
Neither candidate is an obvious final choice yet. The current dhole mark is
useful as the baseline, and changing the UI does not require selecting a name
in the same release. Keep the small mascot distinct from the Lucide navigation
icons, whose job is to identify pages and actions.
